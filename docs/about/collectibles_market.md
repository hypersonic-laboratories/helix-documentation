# Collectibles & Market

## What Are HELIX Collectibles?

Collectibles are universal digital items — clothing, vehicles, accessories, homes, art pieces, and more — that you can use across various HELIX worlds and trade with other players via the HELIX Market.  Most collectibles are **purely cosmetic**.

Think of them like digital sneakers, cars, or artwork: items that let you express identity and style in a shared universe.

---

## Creator Control and Immersion

Every world in HELIX is unique — and so are its rules. World creators and server owners can **choose to disable or restrict certain collectibles** if they don’t fit the setting.

For example, a medieval roleplay server might block modern cars or futuristic outfits to keep immersion intact, while a city world might allow anything.

This flexibility ensures collectibles **enhance** the experience rather than disrupt it.

---

## Why Do They Have Real Prices?

Collectibles are part of the **HELIX Market**, a platform-wide marketplace where users can buy, sell, and trade securely. Prices are set by the community, just like the **Steam Community Market** — HELIX simply provides the infrastructure to make trading safer, faster, and easier. HELIX handles payment, delivery, and fraud protection automatically.

---

## Are Collectibles NFTs or blockchain based?

No, Collectibles are not NFTs and do not incorporate any blockchain technology. 

---

## Are These Required to Play?

Not at all. Collectibles are optional enhancements for players who want to customize their look, support creators, or collect rare items.

---

## A Safer Way to Trade

All trades and purchases go through HELIX’s verified system — no third-party sites, no scams, no lost items. You always know exactly what you’re getting, and your items are safely linked to your HELIX account.

---

## Transparency & Choice

We understand that players have seen too many unfair monetization systems in gaming. HELIX was built to be different — focused on **transparency, ownership, and respect**. Every purchase is optional. Every collectible can be resold. And every item you own is truly yours.
