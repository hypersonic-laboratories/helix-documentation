# LIX

**LIX** is the universal currency of the HELIX platform. It powers the entire creator economy, from world monetization (i.e. **In-World Purchases**) and developer services (i.e. [Instant Hosting](../about/hosting.md)) to [Collectibles](../about/collectibles_market.md) and creator payouts.

LIX keeps everything inside HELIX simple, unified, and secure. Whether you're a player buying a cosmetic or a developer selling a package, LIX is how value moves through the ecosystem.

---

## How LIX Works

Players can purchase LIX using real currency from the HELIX website.

LIX can be used to:

- Partake in in-world transactions through **in-world purchases**
- Pay for **server hosting**, **persistent storage**, and platform services
- Buy **collectibles** on the **HELIX Market**

All transactions occur within the HELIX ecosystem securely and leave a clear audit trail to prevent fraud or abuse; no external wallets, no third-party marketplaces.

---

## Earning LIX as a Creator

Creators earn LIX through:

- Monetizing their **worlds and servers**
- Selling packages on the [HELIX Vault](../about/vault.md)

When someone buys your creation or pays you in-world, the LIX is deposited directly into your account balance as Earned LIX.

---

## Payouts & Platform Fees

Creators can convert **earned LIX** into real currency once they meet eligibility and verification requirements by submitting a payout.

When a payout is issued, HELIX applies a **platform fee**. This fee supports platform operations, payment processing, hosting, security, and creator infrastructure.

**Example:**  
If a creator submits a payout request for 100,000 LIX and the platform fee is 30%, the creator receives **70,000 LIX (~$700 USD)** to their selected payout method (i.e. bank account).

---

## Purchased vs. Earned LIX

To protect the economy and comply with regulations, LIX is separated into two internal categories:

- **Purchased LIX** — bought with real currency.  
  - Can be spent anywhere inside HELIX  
  - **Cannot** be cashed out

- **Earned LIX** — gained through legitimate creator activity.  
  - Eligible for payout conversion

This prevents abuse, fraud, or currency arbitrage while keeping the economy stable and fair.

## Why LIX Exists

LIX makes the HELIX ecosystem:

- **Unified**: one currency across all worlds
- **Fair**: transparent creator earnings and fees
- **Secure**: all transactions handled by HELIX
- **Scalable**: works globally for both players and creators

LIX allows creativity to translate directly into value.

## Compliance & Legal Notes

LIX is a **closed-loop virtual currency**, not a cryptocurrency.  
It is not traded on exchanges and has no blockchain component.

All LIX purchases are final and non-refundable except where required by law.

Payouts are handled through approved partners; creators may need to verify identity and tax information before receiving funds.

## FAQ

### **Is LIX required to play HELIX?**  
No, players and creators can use HELIX for free without ever purchasing LIX. LIX is a purely optional layer.

### **Can I gift LIX to another player?**  
Direct LIX gifting is not supported.

### **Can players cash out LIX?**  
Only **earned LIX** can be converted to real currency. **Purchased LIX** cannot be withdrawn.

### **Does LIX expire?**  
No. LIX remains in your account indefinitely unless required otherwise by regional laws.

### **Can I get a refund for LIX purchases?**  
LIX purchases are non-refundable, except where legally mandated. All LIX spent on collectibles or services is final.

### **What happens if my LIX balance reaches zero?**  
You won’t be able to complete any LIX transactions. Free gameplay and free creator tools remain fully accessible.

### **Is LIX considered a cryptocurrency or token?**  
No. LIX is a traditional virtual currency stored on HELIX servers. It has no blockchain, no gas fees, and no external trading.

### **Do creators get paid automatically?**  
No. Creators must manually request a payout. Verification is required before the first payout.

### **What happens if someone fraudulently buys my content?**  
HELIX has fraud-prevention and chargeback systems. In confirmed cases, the fraudulent buyer is removed and the transaction is reversed internally without penalizing legitimate creators.
