### **⚠️ Known Issues**

- WebUI is not loading files from the correct local filepaths, preventing it from being used entirely. You can use remote URLs, however. This will be fixed in next hotfix release.
- Servers in Main Menu → Servers tab are for internal testing only and will not let you connect. Do not try to connect to them. If you want to join a local server, select “EOS” filter on top left corner to see other user-hosted servers to join.
- Sometimes other players’ movement/positions are out of sync
- Sometimes when connected to listen servers, FPS drops below 10 (related to above issue, might have to do with connected players’ pings)
- Helicopter and plane controls are not responding and the pilot pose animation is incorrect
