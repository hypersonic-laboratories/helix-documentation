# October 13, 2025

### Build \#20251013.8406.335

## 🚀New/Improved Features

### 🎮**Gameplay & Menus**

- Improved menu responsiveness and navigation flow across client interfaces.
- Minor UI polish in settings and start menus.
- Improved input focus handling in HUD and general menu reliability.

---

### 🚗**Vehicles**

- Increased damage sensitivity for helicopters to better reflect impact.
- Fixed and improved collision and glass behaviour for police cars.
- Adjusted vehicle materials for better visual consistency.
- Refined vehicle interaction logic and corrected asset references.
- Fixed police and Prancer vehicle models, restoring missing or broken parts.
- Fixed vehicle explosion visuals and general material rendering issues.

---

### 🎨**Character & Character Creator**

- Continued development on the new Character Creator (Press P to open customizer, or control from Lua API)*.*
- Improved pose, mesh, and preset consistency during customization.
- Added save versioning and temporary local saves to prevent preset loss after updates or disconnections.
- Added new clothing and cosmetic assets, including firefighter and prison outfits, jackets, gloves, and accessories.
- Improved mesh and LOD alignment to reduce clipping and visual glitches.
- Fixed replication and alignment issues affecting gender, age, and control rig behaviour.
- General stability, UI, and data-handling improvements throughout Character Creator.

---

### 🛠️**General Improvements**

- Improved overall stability and replication reliability, reducing desyncs and data issues.
- Enhanced UI responsiveness and transitions across menus and notifications.
- Cleaned up internal dependencies and applied general polish and stability fixes across menus and customization systems.
- Improved workspace initialization and dependency tracking.
- Optimized in-game WebUI rendering to use high-performance GPU on Windows.
- Improved backend version tracking and server validation.

---

### 💻**Lua changes**

- Added error **forwarding and logging from JS to Lua** for better debugging.
- Added JavaScript to Lua callback support.
- Added Lua client to server callback support.
- Introduced QBCore Template World, the world and QBCore Framework are still work-in-progress, currently available functionality: making character, choosing apartment, interacting inside and leaving the apartment, opening city hall menu, selecting and assigning the jobs. Refer to the [QBCore documentation](https://development.helix-documentation.pages.dev/qbcore/) for futher information.
