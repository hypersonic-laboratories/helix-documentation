# Monetize Your World with LIX  

HELIX makes it easy to **monetize your world** through **in-world purchases** powered by **LIX**, the official HELIX currency.  

LIX is a **unified digital currency** used across all worlds for buying, selling, and trading items, services, and collectibles. Players can earn or purchase LIX, and creators can receive payouts from sales made within their worlds.  

By integrating **in-world purchases**, you can charge for **access, items, upgrades, or experiences** directly inside your map — all securely processed through HELIX’s built-in system.  

Using LIX keeps your economy **safe, consistent, and fully connected** to the wider HELIX platform, so your creations can generate real value while players enjoy a seamless experience.
