# Photo Mode

Photo Mode lets you capture cinematic screenshots of your HELIX worlds with full control over the camera, filters, and visual effects.

## 🎮 How to Enter Photo Mode

- Press **`F6`** at any time to toggle Photo Mode.
- Move with **`WASD`**.
- **`Click + Drag Mouse`** to rotate the camera.
- Bottom bar controls:
    - **`X`** → Take Photo
    - **`G`** → Open Album
    - **`Alt`** → Hide/Show Mouse
    - **`F`** → Free Camera
    - **`Tab`**→ Switch category
    - **`R`** → Reset value
    - **`H`** → Hide UI
    - **`Esc`** → Close Photo Mode

## ⚙️ Photo Mode Panels

### 📷 Camera

- Field of View (FOV) – Adjust zoom and perspective
- Roll – Tilt the camera sideways
- Camera Distance – Control offset from the subject
- Resolution – Set output resolution (default = screen resolution)

![image.png](https://r2.fivemanage.com/ElKst3CCVvlhlgHiaH5IM/photomode_1.png)

---

### 💡 Display

- Brightness – Control exposure
- Temperature – Adjust warmth/coolness (Kelvin scale)
- Tint – Fine-tune color balance
- Grid – Toggle reference grid
- Hide Character – Remove your player model from the shot

![image.png](https://r2.fivemanage.com/ElKst3CCVvlhlgHiaH5IM/photomode_2.png)

---

### 🎨 Filter

- Filter – Apply pre-set cinematic filters
- Intensity – Adjust filter strength
- Rendering – Switch rendering styles
- Character Rotation – Rotate your character independently

![image.png](https://r2.fivemanage.com/ElKst3CCVvlhlgHiaH5IM/photomode_3.png)

---

### 🌈 Color Grading

- Color Balance – Switch between **Saturation, Contrast, Hue**, etc.
- Saturation Intensity – Control overall saturation
- Red / Green / Blue – Fine-tune color channels

![image.png](https://r2.fivemanage.com/ElKst3CCVvlhlgHiaH5IM/photomode_4.png)

---

### 🔍 Depth of Field

- Autofocus – Toggle automatic subject focus
- Focus Distance – Manually set focus plane
- Aperture (F-Stop) – Control background blur (lower = blurrier)
- Sensor Width – Simulate different lens sizes

![image.png](https://r2.fivemanage.com/ElKst3CCVvlhlgHiaH5IM/photomode_5.png)

---

### ✨ Screen Effects

- Chromatic Aberration – Lens edge color fringing
- Aberration Offset – Adjust aberration shift
- Sharpness – Control image clarity
- Film Grain – Add cinematic noise
- Vignette – Darken screen edges

![image.png](https://r2.fivemanage.com/ElKst3CCVvlhlgHiaH5IM/photomode_6.png)

---

### 🖼️ Frames

- Frame – Add stylistic borders
- Size – Adjust frame thickness
- Red / Green / Blue – Tint the frame color

![image.png](https://r2.fivemanage.com/ElKst3CCVvlhlgHiaH5IM/photomode_7.png)

---

### 🔖 Logo

- Logo – Overlay a watermark/logo (e.g., HELIX)
- Size – Adjust scale
- Angle – Rotate logo
- Horizontal / Vertical Alignment – Position logo

![image.png](https://r2.fivemanage.com/ElKst3CCVvlhlgHiaH5IM/photomode_8.png)

## 🗂️ Album & Saved Screenshots

All your screenshots are automatically stored in the **Album**:

- Press **G** to open Album
- Review your captured shots
- Re-enter Photo Mode to take more

💾 Screenshots are also saved locally on your PC at: `HELIX\SandboxGame\Screenshots`

---

## 💡 Tips

- Use **Free Camera (F)** for cinematic angles
- Combine **Depth of Field** + **Color Grading** for pro-looking shots
- Hide UI (**H**) before final capture
- Use the **Album** to keep your best collections organized

---

👉 With Photo Mode + Album + local saves, you can easily create, manage, and share high-quality screenshots of your HELIX worlds.
